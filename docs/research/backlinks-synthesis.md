# Backlink skill synthesis

The `backlinks` skill covers external-link decisions as one installable package. It combines profile investigation, editorial acquisition, prospect qualification, launch-site evaluation, and measurement. These workflows share the same placement evidence and tracker. Separate packages would duplicate those contracts.

The existing `seo-engine`, expanded in commit `c573b3c`, covers broader organic-search diagnosis, internal linking, and AI-citation investigation. The new package supplies a focused external-backlink workflow without requiring that skill or changing its instructions.

## Research and choices

Research on 2026-10-24 used skills.sh for candidate discovery and actual source instructions for comparison. Six materially relevant skills were inspected. The portable [source comparison](../../skills/backlinks/references/sources.md) retains exact marketplace links, contemporaneous source revisions, license observations, useful methods, and the changes made here.

Corey Haines's `link-building` supplies the strongest campaign foundation. His `directory-submissions` adds launch-channel qualification and listing verification. Daniel Agrici's `seo-backlinks` contributes data-sufficiency and source-labeling methods. Every's `link-prospecting` adds contribution angles and contact provenance. The shorter `backlink-analysis` supplies a useful audit checklist. The older `backlink-analyzer` contributes output-contract ideas but is marked as moved, so it is only a historical comparison.

The synthesis removes unsupported numeric conversion and citation claims, fixed authority or anchor thresholds, automatic scoring of incomplete profiles, provider-specific paths, and assumptions that more submissions imply better SEO. Instructions and the tracker are original. No upstream code or templates are redistributed, installed, or executed.

## The supplied list

All 50 domains remain in the [dated inventory](../../skills/backlinks/references/launch-sites.md). Public first-party pages were inspected where accessible. Blocked pages remain unverified.

The list is useful for discovery, but it mixes distinct jobs. GitHub and Hashnode support useful projects or publications. Wellfound supports recruiting. Crunchbase supports company information. Review marketplaces, community sites, launch platforms, and directory-submission services each need different eligibility and success measures.

CrazyAboutAI displayed a domain-sale page. Capterra presented a browser verification challenge. There's An AI For That was blocked by browser site-safety policy. The supplied PeerPush `.net` domain redirected to `.com`. A blocked inspection does not establish that a service is defunct.

Many readable sites market authority scores, permanent dofollow links, badges, directory packages, or AI-discovery benefits. Those statements are retained as publisher claims where relevant, not adopted as proof. No new product listing, link attributes, indexing result, referral performance, or customer acquisition was verified in this research.

The package therefore selects channels by product and audience fit, checks current rules, prepares a specific contribution or listing, and evaluates a bounded cohort against its intended outcome. It can expand beyond the supplied list to niche publications, integrations, associations, and local sources.

## Validation boundary

The package includes 15 behavioral scenarios and a CSV opportunity-tracker template. The scenarios cover partial exports, platform rules, misleading offers, lost links, disavow, attribution, missing contacts, hostile source data, and unrelated-task routing. These checks evaluate agent decisions and reporting. They cannot demonstrate ranking gains or real campaign effectiveness.
