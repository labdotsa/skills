# Creative playbook

## Seven practitioner principles

Scrnsht Studio's 2026 article presents seven useful principles based on its reported project experience. Treat these as a creative review lens, not platform law or proven universal conversion effects because the article does not disclose variants, samples, metrics, confidence, or underlying results. See [How To Make High-Converting App Store Screenshots For Your App (7 Principles)](https://x.com/scrnshtstudio/status/2090816024585089129).

1. **Start with a clear value proposition.** Derive the opener from audience research, pain, differentiation, and alternatives before designing.
2. **Sell the outcome, not the feature.** Name the change the user wants while keeping it supported by the adjacent product state.
3. **One screenshot, one message.** Establish one dominant idea and make secondary material support it.
4. **Make the idea obvious.** Use the no-copy test; seek a visual hook that communicates before the headline is read.
5. **Humanize the experience.** Add a face, hand, or real situation where it clarifies context. The article says human-centered concepts often perform better but publishes no supporting results; test this by category and audience.
6. **Build trust.** Use only truthful, current, permissioned ratings, reviews, awards, user counts, press references, research, and affiliations.
7. **Keep copy simple.** Use short, large, natural, nontechnical language. Preserve necessary qualification even when it makes the line longer.

The article's concept boards use oversized headlines, enlarged or composited UI, human context, and trust devices. Treat exact figures, speed comparisons, quotations, third-party logos, awards, ratings, and research references in any concept as unverified until the app owner substantiates and permissions them.

## Category adjustments

### Finance, investing, payments, and insurance

Lead with clarity, control, or the primary money job rather than riches. Show realistic balances, transactions, budgets, fees, security controls, or portfolio states with personal data scrubbed. Place trust and qualifications early. Treat return, savings, approval, security, and superiority claims as evidence-sensitive.

### Health, medical, wellbeing, and fitness

Show the action-feedback loop: plan, activity, measurement, progress, coaching, or care coordination. Distinguish wellness from clinical capability. Use human aspiration without hiding the actual experience. Substantiate diagnosis, treatment, weight, speed, and outcome claims.

### Productivity, utilities, and AI

Demonstrate input-to-output transformation. Show the object created, effort removed, or workflow integrated. Make human control visible for AI and avoid staging a result that appears guaranteed.

### Education and language learning

Show authentic practice, feedback, explanation, progression, or teacher/social interaction. Prefer the searched language or learning job over a generic content catalog. Substantiate fluency, grade, and speed claims.

### Travel, mobility, delivery, and local services

Use locally relevant destinations, inventory, maps, pricing/ETA mechanics, offline support, or coordination. Target custom pages by origin, destination, supply type, or trip purpose when acquisition intent differs.

### Social, dating, creator, and community

Explain the interaction model, content formats, community specificity, and safety controls. Use people and content with permission and avoid exposing private data.

### Games

Treat gameplay as proof. Lead with the core loop, genre, fantasy, or recognized IP. Follow with modes, progression, collection, events, or social play. Keep cinematic art from implying gameplay the product does not provide.

## Failure patterns

- Branding-only opener: consumes the highest-visibility position without resolving intent.
- Feature soup: forces users to translate internal nouns into personal value.
- Tiny UI inside oversized device art: proves an app exists but withholds product evidence.
- Repeated generic promises: creates no cumulative argument.
- Dependent panorama: breaks under cropping, reuse, reordered display, or preview precedence.
- Lifestyle art without product proof: sells mood while leaving the experience ambiguous.
- Unsupported superiority or numerical claims: create policy, legal, and credibility risk.
- English overlays on localized pages: reduce comprehension and signal weak market commitment.
- Direct translation: breaks idiom, hierarchy, line length, RTL direction, and local meaning.
- Install-only optimization: can reward overpromising and reduce activation, retention, revenue, or review quality.
