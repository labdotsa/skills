# Platform surface behavior and evidence

Check the current official pages because store surfaces and experimentation capabilities change. Record the check date. Invoke `submit-mobile-app-stores` for exact dimensions, formats, device wells, packaging, upload validation, and current submission requirements.

## Apple App Store

- Expect the first one to three screenshots to appear in search when no app preview is present. Use them to communicate the app's essence; focus later images on main benefits or features. See [Creating your product page](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/).
- Account for app previews preceding screenshots regardless of their order in App Store Connect. See [Upload app previews and screenshots](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/upload-app-previews-and-screenshots).
- Keep metadata accurate, current, representative, and free of unverifiable claims or real personal account data. Show the app in use rather than only login, splash, or title art. Disclose content requiring additional purchase. See [App Review Guidelines §2.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- Use product-page optimization to test eligible alternate icons, screenshots, and previews. It can compare up to three treatments; interpret Apple's estimated lift and reliability rather than raw point differences alone. See [PPO overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-product-page-optimization-tests/overview-of-product-page-optimization).
- Use custom product pages for audience-, keyword-, campaign-, or content-matched experiences. Apple currently supports up to 70 pages with distinct screenshots, previews, promotional text, and keywords. See [Configure custom product pages](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-custom-product-pages/configure-multiple-product-page-versions).

## Google Play

- For creative eligible for larger recommendation formats, prioritize recognizable, actual UI in the first three and demonstrate core features or gameplay. Validate the current asset requirements during submission preparation.
- Keep taglines restrained and legible. Avoid repetitive images, obsolete devices, non-Android references, unauthorized third-party marks, store badges, notification clutter, distortion, and unlocalized overlays. Wear OS requires UI-only screenshots without added frames, backgrounds, or text.
- Add useful screenshot alt text. Describe meaningful content concisely rather than repeating “image of.”
- Use store-listing experiments for localized text and graphics. Google recommends testing one asset at a time when causal learning matters and running at least one week to cover weekday/weekend variation. Results can include acquisition and one-day retention. See [Store-listing experiments](https://play.google.com/console/about/store-listing-experiments/).
- Use custom store listings for country, user/buyer state, keyword, ad group, or URL-targeted traffic. Google currently supports up to 50. See [Create custom store listings](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867158?hl=en).

## Evidence hierarchy

Use these labels:

1. **Platform rule** — current official requirement or product behavior; strong for compliance, not conversion.
2. **Platform recommendation** — official creative guidance; useful for eligibility and intended experience, not a guaranteed lift.
3. **First-party measured case** — platform- or developer-owned result; directional unless the design, allocation, sample, and confidence are disclosed.
4. **Practitioner guidance** — an experienced operator's reported method; treat performance claims as hypotheses when methods and results are unpublished.
5. **Direct listing observation** — evidence of what a publisher displays now; never proof of conversion.
6. **Synthesis/hypothesis** — a reasoned recommendation requiring a test for the specific app, audience, locale, and source.

Never call a listing “high converting” from rankings, ratings, download bands, ad spend, polish, or category prominence. Neither Apple nor Google publishes a universal cross-category screenshot-conversion dataset.

## Measured examples and their limits

- Apple's documented Simply Piano product-page test found its screenshot-first/no-preview control beat a tested preview-first treatment by 3.3% at reported 100% confidence. This rejects one treatment, not preview video generally. See [Apple PPO Tech Talk](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/110349/).
- The same Apple talk reports Angry Birds 2's evergreen first screenshot beat a holiday treatment by 1.5% at reported 100% confidence. This is creative- and audience-specific, not an argument against seasonal art.
- Apple's custom-page talk reports gains for targeted State of Survival, CBS Sports, Baidu, and Otto pages. These campaign cohorts were not equivalent to randomized PPO tests, so traffic selection and concurrent changes limit causal attribution. See [Custom product pages Tech Talk](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/110361/).
- Google-hosted Tapps Games and Splendid Apps case studies support continuous experimentation and localization, but bundle multiple creative and business changes. Do not assign all reported business lift to screenshots alone. See [Tapps Games](https://play.google.com/console/about/tapps-casestudy/?hl=EN-GB) and [Splendid Apps](https://play.google.com/console/about/splendid-casestudy/).
