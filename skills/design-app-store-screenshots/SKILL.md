# Design app store screenshots

Turn a product truth into a scannable, evidence-backed store narrative. Treat the screenshot set as a segmented acquisition surface, not a feature gallery: match intent, state an outcome, prove it with representative UI, remove uncertainty, and measure both conversion and downstream user quality.

## Establish the assignment

Identify:

- store, device families, locales, and whether the target is a default or custom listing;
- app, category, maturity, monetization, regulated or sensitive claims, and current release state;
- audience, traffic source, search/campaign intent, alternatives, desired action, and principal objection;
- requested branch: **create**, **audit**, **localize**, or **experiment**;
- available product captures, brand system, research, reviews, analytics, claims evidence, and prior tests.

Discover supplied facts and assets before questioning the user. Record consequential gaps instead of filling them with invented product capabilities, users, ratings, awards, testimonials, results, or market evidence. If the assignment can proceed with explicit hypotheses, do so and label them.

Read [platform-surface-behavior.md](references/platform-surface-behavior.md) for every assignment. Read [creative-playbook.md](references/creative-playbook.md) when creating, rewriting, or auditing creative. Read [working-templates.md](references/working-templates.md) when producing a brief, storyboard, audit, or experiment plan.

## Build the message strategy

Write a one-sentence proposition:

> For **audience in context**, the app helps achieve **specific outcome** through **credible differentiator**, unlike **relevant alternative**.

Then create a claim ledger. For every proposed message, record the claim, intended audience, supporting product state, substantiation, qualification, locale, owner, and expiry risk. Classify it as verified fact, supported interpretation, creative hypothesis, or prohibited/unusable until evidenced.

Choose the carousel argument from the prospect's decisions:

1. Is this for me?
2. What outcome do I get?
3. Why should I believe it?
4. Why this app rather than an alternative?
5. Will it feel easy, safe, and appropriate?
6. What additional value rewards a deeper look?

Change the order when category intent demands it. Put trust earlier for finance, health, security, and unfamiliar brands. Lead games with representative gameplay or recognizable intellectual property. Lead targeted listings with the promise that brought the visitor there.

Completion criterion: every selected frame has a distinct persuasion job and every consequential claim has visible proof or an explicit evidence gap.

## Storyboard the set

Give each frame one dominant message. A strong default sequence is:

1. audience-matched outcome and product proof;
2. ease, mechanism, or immediate payoff;
3. meaningful differentiator;
4. representative workflow or depth;
5. category-appropriate trust, safety, or control;
6. breadth, advanced value, community, or ecosystem.

For each frame specify: persuasion job, headline, optional support, product state, visual hook, focal crop, qualification, and transition to the next frame. Make every frame understandable independently; store surfaces may crop, reorder, precede, or reuse assets outside the full carousel.

Write outcome-led headlines in familiar user language. Prefer one short, concrete idea over feature labels, category platitudes, keyword stuffing, install commands, or clever ambiguity. Use copy to sharpen a visible concept rather than rescue an unintelligible visual. Run the **no-copy test**: hide every overlay and verify that the remaining product state still communicates the rough idea.

Completion criterion: the first frame resolves audience or intent and outcome at thumbnail scale; later frames advance rather than repeat the argument.

## Direct the visual system

Compose every frame from five layers:

1. **message** — one dominant claim;
2. **proof** — current, privacy-safe, representative UI;
3. **focus** — scale, crop, contrast, or restrained annotation;
4. **brand** — a consistent but subordinate visual language;
5. **continuity** — rhythm across the set without dependence on adjacent frames.

Enlarge the product state enough to inspect. Populate it with realistic data. Keep annotations distinguishable from controls. Use people, hands, or real-life context only when they clarify the job or help the audience imagine use; treat human-first versus UI-first as a hypothesis, not a universal conversion rule. Use trust signals only when current, auditable, permissioned, and legible.

Design store-specific exports rather than blindly resizing one master. Check thumbnail comprehension, safe zones, crop resilience, status bars, device accuracy, contrast, text size, color-independent meaning, translation expansion, right-to-left direction, and Google Play alt text.

Completion criterion: every frame remains truthful, legible, and useful at its smallest expected placement and in every requested locale/device variant.

## Audit existing screenshots

Score each dimension from 0 to 2 using the rubric in [working-templates.md](references/working-templates.md): intent match, first-frame proof, hierarchy, sequence, differentiation, fidelity, trust, localization, accessibility, and testability.

Report observations separately from recommendations. For every issue state:

- the visible evidence;
- the user or business consequence;
- the proposed change;
- the claim or assumption it depends on;
- its priority and a testable hypothesis.

Do not infer conversion performance from polish, ratings, rankings, install bands, or competitor adoption. Describe live listings as observable creative practice unless first-party experimental evidence supports a performance claim.

Completion criterion: every score has evidence and every material recommendation has a reason, dependency, and verification method.

## Localize the proposition

Transcreate rather than substitute words. Reassess the audience job, promise, proof, content examples, currencies, dates, maps, names, legal qualification, line length, reading direction, and cultural meaning for each locale. Preserve message priority instead of forcing identical line breaks or composition.

Use custom product pages or custom store listings when campaign, keyword, country, buyer state, or audience intent deserves a different proposition—not merely a translated one.

Completion criterion: each locale communicates the intended outcome naturally, uses locally valid product evidence, and passes layout and claims review.

## Design the experiment

Write the hypothesis before producing variants:

> For **audience/source/locale**, changing **one defined variable** from **A** to **B** will improve **primary outcome**, because **evidence-backed reason**; reject it if **quality or value guardrail** worsens.

Prefer one conceptual variable per causal-learning test: opener, message, UI scale, order, trust placement, human context, or preview presence. Record control, treatment, allocation, audience, duration, stopping rule, store confidence output, and diagnostic cuts. Measure install/download conversion with activation, retention, subscription/purchase, refund, review, or other fit-sensitive guardrails when available.

Treat complete redesigns as challenger tests that can identify a winner but cannot isolate the cause. Replicate surprising wins and monitor after rollout because audience and season change.

Completion criterion: the plan can identify a winner without rewarding misleading acquisition or claiming causality the design cannot support.

## Deliver

Return the artifacts appropriate to the branch:

- strategy brief and claim ledger;
- ordered storyboard with copy and visual direction;
- store/device/locale asset matrix;
- substantiation and policy-risk register;
- scored audit with prioritized revisions;
- experiment plan with variants, metrics, guardrails, and decision rule;
- unresolved inputs and owners.

Separate **verified facts**, **practitioner guidance**, **observed patterns**, and **hypotheses** in the final output. Cite current official store documentation for platform rules and state the date checked.

## Hand off for submission

Invoke `submit-mobile-app-stores` when screenshot assets must be resized to exact store specifications, mapped to required device wells, packaged with listing metadata, validated in a submission dossier or live console, uploaded, submitted for review, or released. Pass the approved creative set, source files, locale/device intent, asset matrix, claim ledger, and unresolved qualifications. Retain creative ownership here; transfer submission validation and external mutations there.

## Done when

- Every requested store, device family, audience, and locale is accounted for.
- Every frame has one persuasion job, one dominant message, and representative product proof.
- Every factual or comparative claim is substantiated, qualified, or removed.
- The first frame works at thumbnail scale and the set forms a cumulative argument.
- Accessibility, localization, fidelity, and platform constraints are checked.
- Recommendations do not masquerade as conversion evidence.
- Experiments include downstream quality guardrails and an explicit decision rule.
