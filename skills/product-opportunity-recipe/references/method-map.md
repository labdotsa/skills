# Method routing map

Activate the smallest set of methods whose native outputs resolve distinct decision-blocking uncertainties. When subjects overlap, route to the method whose native output answers the blocker; compose only when distinct blockers require multiple native outputs. Each method keeps its native output and validation.

| Method | Activate when | Required entry | Native output | Do not use it to claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| iso-56007-opportunity-validation | raw idea needs front-end governance | innovation intent and sponsor candidate | Opportunity Evaluation Charter | solution feasibility or investment approval |
| market-research-design | sampling, instrument, or fieldwork design must be fixed or audited | decision candidate, owner candidate, and proposed/active/completed research materials | Research Protocol or as-executed audit | findings before collection or retrospective work as preregistered |
| evidence-triangulation | provenance, contradiction, or confidence across sources blocks the decision | claims plus sources or native records | Evidence system | certainty from source count |
| market-sizing | count/volume/value changes the decision | market estimand and boundary | Market Sizing Model | customer desire or attainability without capacity |
| market-segmentation | groups may require different action | market boundary and discriminatory evidence | Segment Map | personas or prevalence without fit data |
| pestle-analysis | external change may alter strategy | geography, subject, horizon | external-environment system | industry profitability by itself |
| porters-five-forces | industry structure/profit matters | industry and value-chain boundary | structural thesis | firm-level competitor positioning |
| competitive-analysis | customer choice/difference matters | customer choice event/segment | Competitive Landscape | whole-industry profit potential |
| customer-discovery | problem, behavior, roles, access unknown | hypotheses and recruitable cases | Customer Discovery Dossier | population prevalence from qualitative cases |
| jobs-to-be-done | switching/hiring dynamics matter | recent choice episodes | JTBD Map | feature priority from opinions |
| assumption-mapping | concept beliefs need prioritization | live concept and intended outcome | Assumption Map/Test Queue | experiment result |
| pretotyping | consequential demand can be tested before build | one testable behavioral assumption | Behavioral Experiment Record | technical feasibility or full product demand |
| value-proposition-canvas | jobs, pains, gains, and proposed value mechanisms need explicit fit for one context | one bounded customer context and evidence | Value Proposition Canvas | business-model viability |
| business-model-canvas | value/delivery/capture architecture needs design | evidence-backed segment/value hypothesis | Business Model Options | validated demand by canvas completion |
| pricing-research | price level, metric, packaging, structure, or demand response is decision-blocking | defined offer, payer, and pricing question or a branch that will establish them | Pricing Evidence Report | revealed willingness from stated methods alone |
| market-entry-strategy | geography/channel/partner commitment needs sequencing | bounded beachhead and offer | Market Entry Strategy | customer value without discovery |
| opportunity-solution-tree | continuous discovery choices need a shared tree | outcome, customer/value theory, story evidence | discovery decision surface | demand or opportunities invented by team |
| unido-feasibility-study | defined option needs integrated pre-investment test | sponsor/decision, option boundary, and enough rough design inputs to create a design basis | Integrated Feasibility Study | vague concept feasibility |
| unit-economics | marginal/cohort economics matter | candidate economic unit plus available revenue, cost, acquisition, retention, cash, or capacity drivers | Unit Economics Model | organization-wide cash or funding alone |
| financial-modeling | cash, statements, funding, returns matter | decision outputs, entity/project boundary, horizon, and available driver/source materials | financial package | truth beyond input evidence |
| iso-31000-risk-assessment | a defined objective/option needs treatment and acceptance | objective, option/scope, stakeholder/authority candidates, and available risk evidence | risk system | feasibility or economics by risk scoring |
| five-case-model | integrated commitment decision is due | current mandatory handoffs and real options | Product Investment Case | readiness when dependencies are stale |

## Shared-boundary handoff

At composition time—not inside each method—reconcile the dimensions each pair shares:

| Handoff | Reconcile |
| --- | --- |
| sizing → segmentation | population, unit, geography, period |
| segmentation → discovery | eligibility and reach |
| discovery → JTBD/VPC | episode IDs, customer context, roles |
| assumptions → pretotype | exact claim, population, threshold |
| VPC → BMC | segment, value mechanism, proof burden |
| BMC/pricing/entry | payer, value metric, channel, price, contract |
| feasibility → unit economics | volume, capacity, unit cost, schedule |
| unit economics → finance | cohorts, price, contribution, cash timing |
| feasibility/finance → risk | conditions, scenarios, controls, consequences |
| all current artifacts → Five Case | option set, assumptions, dates, owners |

Specialist legal, regulatory, privacy, cybersecurity, safety, AI-evaluation, marketplace-liquidity, manufacturing, supply-chain, or environmental analysis enters only when a named decision question requires it.
