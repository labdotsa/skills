# Backlinks

Turn backlink evidence into a qualified opportunity queue, a concrete contribution or submission, and a way to measure its value. Choose the workflow the request needs. A directory launch does not require a full backlink audit.

Keep four outcomes distinct: earned editorial links, referral traffic and customers, launch exposure and feedback, and accurate business or review profiles. A placement can help one outcome without helping the others. A submitted form, published listing, observed link, and measured conversion are different states.

## Establish the decision

Recover existing context before asking questions. Identify the product and domain, target pages, audience, language and geography, launch stage, desired outcome, competitors, available assets, budget, time, and permissions. Ask only for missing details that change the recommendation. Without product context, provide a conditional shortlist rather than declare universal winners.

For a ranking request, check whether target-page relevance, search intent, crawlability, index eligibility, or an unsuitable offer is a stronger known constraint than links. Do not diagnose a backlink deficit from domain age or a low vendor score alone. A referral or beta-feedback campaign can still be useful without an indexable launch page.

Use available first-party exports, backlink providers, public pages, and analytics. No named subscription, connector, model, browser, or companion skill is required. Discover actual capabilities before requesting data. Do not install tools or buy access merely to complete the analysis.

## Work from evidence

Record the exact source URL or export, observation date, provider and database when applicable, coverage, and what the evidence supports. Separate observed facts, publisher claims, estimates, hypotheses, and unknowns. A founder recommendation supplies a candidate, not proof of traffic, safety, current availability, or ranking value.

Inspect representative placements from the same listing type or paid tier before assigning link properties to an opportunity. After publication, inspect the actual placement. Record its URL, visible context, anchor text, destination, redirect destination, and actual `rel` tokens. Record access failures as blocked. A homepage cannot establish a listing's link behavior.

An ordinary link needs no `dofollow` attribute. Absence of `nofollow` does not establish indexing, ranking credit, or future permanence. `nofollow`, `ugc`, and `sponsored` links can still produce useful referrals. Keep page accessibility, index eligibility, observed index membership, and link-provider discovery separate. Unknown values stay unknown.

Prioritize audience and topic fit, a concrete reason for inclusion, destination usefulness, editorial quality, evidence confidence, effort, and total cost. Explain the ordering. Vendor DR, DA, toxicity scores, and sitewide traffic estimates are supporting observations, not Google metrics or acceptance thresholds. Do not manufacture a numeric quality score from missing data.

Use `pursue`, `investigate`, `defer`, or `reject` for each assessed opportunity, with its reason and next action. An unverified prospect can remain worth investigating. It cannot become a verified acquired link through a favorable score.

## Prepare, execute, and verify

1. State the chosen outcome, scope, evidence limits, and deliverable. Preserve the supplied list before filtering it.
2. Complete the selected workflow. Match each opportunity to a target page and a useful contribution, listing, or launch reason.
3. Prepare the smallest reviewable submission pack or outreach draft. Check current destination rules, including relevant authorship restrictions. If an unresolved restriction prevents establishing compliance, record it as unknown and provide factual notes or a human-author checklist for that route.
4. Execute external actions only within the user's authorization for the action and destination. Reuse existing authorization. An analysis request permits research and drafts, not account creation, purchases, messages, posts, uploads, badge installation, or disavow uploads.
5. Verify the actual delivered state and record outcomes separately from activity. Give unresolved items an owner and a review date or trigger.

Stop a route that requires access, payment, or authority outside the agreed scope. Continue independent research and draft preparation. Do not bypass challenges or substitute an unapproved service. For outreach, agree on a bounded contact and follow-up scope. Stop on a decline, opt-out, platform restriction, or exhausted scope.

Keep the tracker at placement level. [opportunities.csv](assets/opportunities.csv) is an optional opportunity template. Keep export metadata and coverage in the source ledger described by the audit workflow, then join through `source_record`. Distinguish `candidate`, `qualified`, `drafted`, `submitted`, `accepted`, `live`, `link_verified`, `declined`, `lost`, and `blocked`. These states need not occur in order. Record a separately discovered editorial link without inventing a submission history.

## Preserve editorial integrity

Offer original evidence, useful tools, corrections, relevant expertise, or accurate product facts. Let editors choose whether and how to link. Use natural descriptive anchors. Write to the audience and the publisher's rules rather than impose exact-match anchor quotas.

Exclude purchased ranking credit, private blog networks, automated link creation, concealed reciprocal schemes, fabricated reviews, vote trading, covert promotion, and mass comment or forum spam. Disclose affiliation. Evaluate legitimate advertising on audience and commercial outcomes, with appropriate `sponsored` or `nofollow` qualification. A badge exchanged for a ranking link requires scrutiny, not automatic installation.

For review platforms, distinguish a vendor profile from a customer review. Never invent customer experience, gate requests to happy customers, or require positive sentiment. Verify the platform's current policy before any incentive proposal. Do not invent contact addresses or send the same pitch to every site.

Do not recommend disavowing links merely because a vendor labels them toxic or low authority. Apply the specific evidence and manual-action conditions in [audit-and-measurement.md](references/audit-and-measurement.md).

## Deliver the decision

Scale the output to the request. A narrow site-list assessment can be one table. A campaign needs a plan and tracker. Retain:

- The business outcome, target pages, baseline, scope, sources, and material unknowns.
- A ranked queue with a decision and reason for every assessed candidate. Account for excluded, duplicate, and blocked entries.
- For each selected opportunity, its audience fit, contribution angle, rules, destination, costs, owner, authority, and next action.
- Reviewable drafts where requested, followed by exact live-placement evidence only when publication actually happened.
- A measurement window, next review, and stop or continuation rule based on useful outcomes and cost.

Finish when the requested scope is accounted for and the next action is executable. Separate completion of research, preparation, execution, and measured results. Never promise rankings, AI citations, revenue, or a universal number of links.

Evaluate revisions with [evals/scenarios.md](evals/scenarios.md). Those cases test decisions and boundaries, not real-world ranking gains.
