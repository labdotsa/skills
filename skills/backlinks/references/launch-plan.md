# Plan directory and startup launches

## Turn a list into channel decisions

Treat a supplied launch list as an inventory of leads. Preserve its entries and order in the evidence record. Check duplicates, aliases, changed purposes, parked domains, submission availability, and eligibility. A failed browser challenge means blocked verification, not a dead site.

The [50-site inventory](launch-sites.md) is a dated seed list. It preserves the supplied domains with observations from 2026-10-24. It is neither a trust list nor a recommendation to submit everywhere. Recheck selected sites and terms before execution. Broaden the search to relevant niche resources, associations, integrations, and editorial publications when the supplied list misses the audience.

Classify the channel before planning the work:

| Channel | Useful objective | Qualification question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch platform | Feedback and early-adopter discovery | Are its active readers plausible users, and can the founder support a launch? |
| Community or editorial site | Useful discussion or news coverage | Does this specific community permit the contribution and its promotional content? |
| Product, niche, or alternatives directory | Discovery by people comparing tools | Does the listing match a real category or alternative and help an actual buying decision? |
| Review marketplace | Accurate vendor presence and authentic customer evidence | Is the product eligible, and are there real customers who can review it under the current policy? |
| Publishing or developer platform | Useful articles, code, documentation, or projects | Is there an independently useful publication or repository to maintain? |
| Company or recruiting profile | Accurate business identity, research, or hiring | Does the company need this profile for the platform's real purpose? |
| Submission agency or aggregator | Paid administrative service | What exact destinations, terms, account ownership, deliverables, and total costs are included? |

Do not treat an agency's package as one directory placement. Do not create empty GitHub repositories, irrelevant blog posts, or fabricated hiring activity to obtain links.

## Verify the selected channels

For each candidate, record the official rule or submission URL, observation date, product and audience fit, available submission route, moderation, language and stage constraints, asset requirements, current free or paid terms, renewal, queue, and attribution options. Unknown fees remain unknown.

Distinguish payment for review or faster scheduling from a promise to sell ranking credit. Inspect the actual arrangement, not just the label. For any paid placement, require appropriate link qualification. Assess the audience value before considering spend.

Check badge and reciprocal-link conditions. Record the outbound link, visibility, maintenance, and eligibility requirements. A free listing can still have an implementation cost or a link-exchange condition. Do not recommend a badge network solely to exchange ranking signals.

Inspect an example listing of the same type and tier. Record what is observed about its destination and link, while keeping the proposed product's eventual link unverified. Publisher DR, visitor counts, permanence, indexing promises, and AI-discovery claims remain publisher claims unless independently measured.

Choose `pursue`, `investigate`, `defer`, or `reject` with a product-specific reason. Prioritize audience fit and a useful page over the length of the list. Set the first cohort size from available effort and the decision to be tested, not an arbitrary backlink quota. Give every supplied entry a disposition when the user requests a full assessment.

## Prepare the submission pack

Reuse truthful product facts and owned assets. Prepare a canonical destination, name, short positioning line, audience-specific description, category, price model, current availability, logo, screenshots, maker identity, support contact, and disclosure when the destination needs them.

Adapt the angle to each audience while keeping factual claims consistent. A developer launch can emphasize the API or working demo. An alternatives directory needs an accurate comparison and differentiator. A review marketplace needs a correct vendor profile. Do not create generic alternatives pages purely to support listings, or invent users, testimonials, review experience, revenue, or partnerships.

Validate exact field limits and asset specifications on the current form. Use placeholders for missing facts in drafts and name the owner who must supply them. Do not submit invented values. Keep private contact details out of public descriptions unless their publication is authorized.

Where the product already has a useful use-case, integration, comparison, or asset page, choose the destination that matches visitor intent. Otherwise use the accurate product page. New landing pages are a separate proposal unless requested.

## Schedule and execute

Check the live submission window and its time zone. Convert it for the founder and record daylight-saving behavior when it matters. Do not turn a historical best-time recommendation into a rule about the current platform algorithm.

Plan who answers questions, tests signup, handles feedback, and fixes material launch problems. Submit only to eligible channels under existing authorization. Do not request votes, buy engagement, coordinate vote rings, or mask the founder's affiliation.

For Hacker News, inspect its current guidelines and Show HN rules when applicable. The guidelines observed during this skill's research prohibit generated or automated posts. Provide a factual preparation checklist instead of a generated submission where that rule applies. For Reddit, inspect the specific subreddit, not only sitewide guidance. A sitewide account does not establish permission to promote in every community.

For review platforms, separate claiming the business profile from soliciting reviews. Use real users and the current invitation policy. Avoid fabricated, selective positive-only, or undisclosed compensated reviews.

For an agency, prepare a reviewable destination list and terms assessment before purchase. Confirm whether submissions use founder-owned accounts, whether paid directory fees are included, which recurring charges apply, and whether the report distinguishes submissions, approvals, and verified live placements. The agency's invoice is not proof that links exist.

## Review the cohort

Revisit accepted and pending submissions at the agreed review date. Verify actual live pages and links, capture referral and conversion evidence when available, and record fees and effort. A rejected or blocked submission remains in the tracker with its reason.

Compare the cohort against the stated feedback, referral, profile, or search objective. Recommend continue, revise, defer, or stop with evidence. Launches without useful outcomes do not become effective because they increased a vendor authority score.
