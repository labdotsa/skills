# The 50-site launch inventory

Observation date: 2026-10-24. These are the supplied candidates in their original order. Public first-party pages were inspected where accessible. A visible service or submission route does not establish its trustworthiness, traffic quality, current acceptance, or backlink value.

The observations support channel classification and next checks. They do not verify a new product's live placement, link attributes, indexing, permanence, or commercial return. Prices and queues change, so this inventory records the relevant condition rather than maintain a price list. Recheck the selected submission form and terms before execution.

`Observed` means the named public service page was readable. `Blocked` means the page could not be inspected. `Parked` means the domain displayed a sale page during this run. These are access observations, not quality scores.
