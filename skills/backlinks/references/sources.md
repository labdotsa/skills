# Sources and synthesis limits

Research date: 2026-10-24. Candidates were discovered on skills.sh and their actual `SKILL.md` contents inspected. Install counts, stars, and marketplace security checks are discovery signals. They do not validate SEO claims, current APIs, or campaign outcomes.

This package combines selected methods in original instructions. It does not vendor upstream scripts, templates, or runtime integrations. No candidate skill was installed or its scripts executed. The source repositories below declare MIT unless otherwise noted.

## Primary search guidance

| Source | Supported decision | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Google link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam) | Exclude purchased ranking credit, excessive exchanges, automated link creation, low-quality directory links, optimized forum spam, and low-value content created to manipulate links | A directory listing or advertising payment is not automatically a violation. Intent and arrangement matter. Qualified advertising is allowed. |
| [Qualify outbound links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links) | `sponsored` for paid placements, acceptable `nofollow` alternative, `ugc` for user contributions, and multiple possible tokens | An ordinary link needs no special `rel`. Observed attributes alone cannot prove ranking credit or referral value. |
| [Search Console Links report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9049606?hl=en) | Sample coverage, canonical grouping, duplicate combination, export limits, historical links, no `nofollow` field, and unavailable values exported as zero | The report is not a complete live-link inventory and cannot establish every link's current attributes. |
| [Google disavow guidance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/2648487?hl=en) | Most sites do not need disavow. Both considerable problematic links and a caused or likely manual action are required. Uploads replace existing lists and do not support Domain properties. | A vendor toxicity label or a ranking decline alone does not establish these conditions. |

The [launch inventory](launch-sites.md) supplies first-party pages for the user's 50 candidates. Those pages establish only the observations recorded there. Marketing claims about DR, permanent links, guaranteed indexing, AI recommendations, or traffic are not independent evidence of effectiveness.

Hacker News and subreddit rules can be stricter than general SEO guidance. Inspect the exact community's current rules, including its policy on generated content, before preparing a contribution. Platform rules and terms take priority over a generic outreach template for that destination.

## What this research does not establish

No live customer campaign, paid backlink index, private analytics, or controlled ranking experiment was used. The skill is a method for making and testing decisions. It is not evidence that a listed site will improve rankings, generate customers, raise an authority score, or cause AI citations.

The behavioral scenarios check source handling, channel fit, permission boundaries, and honest reporting. They do not benchmark this package against all existing backlink skills or prove a universally optimal strategy. Reassess the method against actual qualified outcomes and costs as campaign evidence becomes available.
