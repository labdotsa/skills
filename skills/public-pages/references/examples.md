## How to deconstruct a specimen

1. Record URL, date, market, locale, device/account state, and visible experiment clues.
2. Separate observed content and structure from inferred rationale.
3. Identify the page job, conceptual spine, evidence placement, action structure, and recovery paths.
4. Record accessibility, performance, localization, maintenance, and operational obligations created by the pattern.
5. State a transferable hypothesis and the local evidence needed to test it.
6. Never report the specimen as proof of conversion, trust, retention, or revenue.
