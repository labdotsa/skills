# Page models

## Governing brief

Record these fields before choosing copy or layout:

| Field | Required answer |
|---|---|
| Audience | Primary visitor, context, prior knowledge, language, and accessibility needs |
| Intent | Question or job that brought the visitor here |
| Promise | Concrete outcome, scope, and qualification |
| Evidence | Product specimen, mechanism, customer evidence, number, policy, comparison, or documentation needed for belief |
| Objections | Material reasons a suitable visitor may not proceed |
| Primary action | Next meaningful step and resulting state |
| Alternatives | Correct paths for visitors who are unready, unsupported, or out of scope |
| Success | Task and downstream-quality outcomes |
| Guardrails | Complaints, refunds, cancellation, accessibility failures, performance, privacy, security, and segment harm |
| Constraints | Legal, operational, technical, localization, brand, and maintenance limits |
| Ownership | Source of truth, approver, page owner, review date, and change triggers |

Use `orient → demonstrate → substantiate → resolve → act or recover` as a diagnostic narrative, not a mandatory visual template. A visitor may enter through pricing, comparison, documentation, support, or an error route and require a different order.

## Shared page contract

- Give each page one dominant job and a small number of genuinely different secondary routes.
- Make title, H1, opening promise, source message, and primary action agree.
- Use a meaningful semantic outline, descriptive real links, stable navigation, and a useful recovery/trust footer.
- Put decision-critical facts and qualifications beside the decision.
- Prefer low information cost over arbitrary shortness. Route evaluators to deeper evidence without hiding essential facts.
- Pair every consequential claim with the strongest available evidence and its limitations.
- Design the entire state space: default, focus, hover, pressed, disabled, loading, empty, success, validation failure, submission failure, expired, reduced motion, long translation, RTL, slow network, and dependency failure.

## Page-family contracts

| Family | Primary job | Must answer or show | Common failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home or hub | Orient mixed visitors and route them | What this is, for whom, differentiated outcome, representative proof, major routes, trust, and 1–2 distinct actions | Abstract hero or an undifferentiated whole-company scroll |
| Campaign landing | Continue a source promise | Message match, offer, qualification, evidence, material terms, focused action, and risk-appropriate fallback | Removing exits while hiding context; optimizing raw leads |
| Product or service | Explain fit and mechanism | Audience, job, outcome, workflow, requirements, limits, integrations, security, pricing, and next step | Feature soup and decorative screenshots |
| Feature or capability | Resolve one narrow evaluation question | Workflow, prerequisites, compatibility, limits, evidence, parent product, docs, and next step | Thin search doorway or unexplained UI |
| Use case or industry | Translate capability into a real context | Actor, workflow, constraints, relevant outcomes, evidence, and implementation route | Search-and-replace vertical copy or stereotypes |
| Pricing or plans | Support comparison and buying mode | Currency, unit, cadence, limits, overages, add-ons, taxes/fees, renewal, cancellation, plan fit, full comparison, and contact/self-serve paths | Hidden meters, annual-price ambiguity, or checkout mismatch |
| About | Establish identity and accountability | Purpose, origin, current facts, accountable people or ownership, dated milestones, press, careers, and contact | Manifesto without verifiable facts |
| Contact or sales | Route a request and set expectations | Channel, geography/language, availability, response time, requested data and purpose, privacy, and support/emergency alternative | One long form with no routing or confirmation |
| Signup or account creation | Create access with minimum justified friction | Value, minimal fields, identity choices, password-manager support, terms at commitment, error recovery, and verification next step | Forced opt-in, late constraints, blocked paste, or fragile JavaScript-only flow |
| Documentation–marketing hybrid | Help an evaluator complete a real task | Task routes, concept plus executable path, prerequisites/version, examples, errors, limits, references, and support | Marketing where an answer should be; stale snippets |
| Article or resource | Answer an information need | Specific question, original value, author/source, freshness, related next steps, and honest scope | Ranking-first filler, artificial word count, or date laundering |
| Trust, security, or privacy | Support due diligence | Scope, controls, boundaries, audit period, subprocessors, incidents/status, policies, contacts, and disclosure route | Unqualified compliance or security superlatives |
| Careers or job | Help candidates assess and apply | Role, location, arrangement, responsibilities, requirements, compensation where applicable, process, accessibility, and truthful status | Ghost roles, discriminatory assumptions, or inaccessible application |
| Error, unavailable, or expired | Explain reality and enable recovery | What happened, whether retry is safe, alternatives, support/status, and preservation of entered work where possible | Soft 404, blanket-home redirect, blame, or a broken app shell |

## Scan-completeness test

Read only the title, H1, opening paragraph, headings, CTA labels, prices, captions, and form labels. A new visitor should be able to answer:

1. What is this?
2. Is it for me?
3. What is meaningfully different?
4. Why should I believe it?
5. What does it cost or require?
6. What happens if I act?

Treat a failed answer as a structural defect, not merely a copy edit.
