# Jobs to Be Done

Study a choice in motion. A job is the progress a person seeks in particular circumstances; the product is one candidate hire.

## Admit only useful cases

Prefer participants with a recent:

- purchase, switch, adoption, or cancellation;
- workaround or combination of alternatives;
- active search that ended in no decision;
- repeated use or abandonment after first use.

Record the hire being studied and the episode date. If the participant can discuss only general preferences or an imagined future, route the case to exploratory discovery and exclude it from a switch synthesis.

## Reconstruct the switch timeline

Interview from memory anchors rather than abstract “why” questions:

| Timeline stage | What to recover |
| --- | --- |
| First thought | First moment the current way felt inadequate |
| Passive looking | Incidental information, conversations, or comparisons |
| Trigger event | What made action harder to postpone |
| Active looking | Alternatives considered, search criteria, tradeoffs |
| Decision | Who influenced, what anxiety remained, what tipped the choice |
| First use | Setup, surprise, workarounds, confirmation or regret |
| Ongoing use/fire | When it earns repeat use or gets replaced |

Probe for places, people, sequence, artifacts, and changes in emotion or confidence. Distinguish retrospective interpretation from remembered events.

**Timeline complete when:** a third party can explain how the participant moved from old way to new hire without inventing a missing transition.

## Draw the forces of progress

At the decision point, map:

~~~text
          pull of new solution
                 ↑
habit of present ← CHOICE → anxiety of new
                 ↓
          push of situation
~~~

Create a force record:

| Force | Episode evidence | Strength/context | Design or market implication |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Push | What made the old situation unacceptable | | |
| Pull | What outcome or new possibility attracted | | |
| Anxiety | Risk, uncertainty, loss, effort, identity concern | | |
| Habit | Familiar workflow, sunk learning, relationships, inertia | | |

Separate a weak pull from a strong anxiety: they require different responses.

## Name progress at three levels

Derive from episodes:

| Dimension | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Functional | What task or outcome must change? |
| Emotional | How does the person want to feel or avoid feeling? |
| Social | How do they want to be seen or behave with others? |

Write a candidate job:

~~~text
When [circumstance/struggle],
help me [progress],
so I can [desired outcome].
~~~

Check it:

- circumstance is specific enough to predict a choice;
- progress is solution-independent;
- outcome matters to the participant;
- evidence spans more than one relevant episode;
- contradictory or different jobs remain separate.

Avoid jobs so broad that every product qualifies or so narrow that the current product appears in the statement.

## Identify the real competing hires

Build a hiring table:

| Hire/current way | Circumstance hired | Progress delivered | Tradeoffs | Reason fired/retained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include manual work, services, internal teams, combinations, delay, and non-consumption. Products from another category may be the strongest competitor when they serve the same progress.

Distinguish:

- **Big Hire:** decision to bring the solution into the person’s life or organization;
- **Little Hire:** repeated moments in which it is chosen and used.

A successful Big Hire with failed Little Hires is an adoption/retention problem, not proof that the job vanished.

## Synthesize job patterns across cases

Do not jump from memorable timelines to one elegant job. Build a Job Pattern Matrix:

| Episode ID | Circumstance/trigger | Push/pull/anxiety/habit pattern | Competing hires | Functional/emotional/social progress | Big/Little Hire result | Desired outcomes | Contradiction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Cluster only when cases share a predictive circumstance, force pattern, competing-hire set, and progress direction. Merge candidate jobs when their wording differs but they predict the same choice and outcomes. Split them when the hire, anxiety/habit, success criterion, or circumstance would lead to a different product/market action. Keep single-case patterns as candidates and disconfirming cases visible.

Create one pattern record per surviving job with included/excluded episode IDs, common mechanism, variation, contrary cases, and the next case that would merge, split, or reject it.

## Convert episodes into opportunity

Create desired-outcome statements that describe direction without prescribing a feature:

~~~text
minimize / increase + measurable outcome + circumstance
~~~

For each outcome, record importance evidence, current satisfaction, frequency, consequence, segment/circumstance, and confidence. Quantify only with an appropriate follow-on method.

Segment by meaningful circumstances and forces, not by demographic portraits alone.

## Diagnose weak JTBD work

| Symptom | Diagnosis | Repair |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Job names a feature | Solution smuggled into progress | Return to struggle and desired outcome |
| Interview is a preference list | No switching episode | Rebuild chronology from a real choice |
| Competitors are category peers only | Hire boundary too narrow | Ask what was used before and what happens without purchase |
| One elegant job explains every case | Differences averaged away | Split by circumstance, force pattern, or outcome |
| Purchase proves lasting value | Big Hire confused with Little Hire | Examine repeated use, abandonment, and rehire |

## Deliver the JTBD Map

Deliver episode timelines, force diagrams, Job Pattern Matrix and pattern records, functional/emotional/social progress, job statements with evidence, competing-hire table, Big/Little Hire findings, desired outcomes, circumstance-based segments, contradictions, and next tests.

| Status | Controlling pattern-matrix state | Permitted handoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `job_pattern_supported` | Multiple eligible independent episodes share a predictive circumstance/force/hire/progress pattern and contrary cases are bounded | hand off the supported pattern and outcome hypotheses to opportunity/solution work |
| `multiple_jobs_require_partition` | Two or more patterns predict different choices or outcomes and cannot be defensibly merged | keep separate contexts/segments and choose which to investigate |
| `candidate_job_only` | One or too few eligible cases support a plausible job, or the pattern lacks contrast | recruit discriminating cases; make no generalized job claim |
| `insufficient_switch_evidence` | Cases lack reconstructable choice/use episodes or missing transitions prevent force and hire inference | route to eligible switch/stay/abandon/non-consumption recruitment; deliver no completed JTBD Map |

Foundation: [Christensen Institute Jobs to Be Done Theory](https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/).
